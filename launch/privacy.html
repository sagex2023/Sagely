Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This page explains what information SAGE-X, LLC ("we", "us") collects when you visit our website or join our email list, and what we do with it. We try to collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up for the free weekly email. We collect nothing else on the signup form.
  • Email activity, such as whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, if our email service provides it. We use it only to see whether the emails are useful.
  • Messages you send us, if you reply to an email or write to us.

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use it

  • To send you the weekly email and any account or service messages.
  • To answer your replies and questions.
  • To improve the emails, using combined, not personal, numbers where we can.

We do not sell your email address. We do not give it to advertisers.

Who else handles your information

We use Buttondown to store the email list and send email, and GitHub Pages to host this website. They process information for us under their own privacy policies. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Unsubscribing and deleting

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and it works in one click. To have your email address deleted from our list, write to sage.x.llc@gmail.com and we will do it. Some records may remain for a short time in backups.

Keeping it safe

We use reputable services and limit who can see the list. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have rights to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Write to sage.x.llc@gmail.com to ask.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date above. If the change is big, we will tell you by email.
